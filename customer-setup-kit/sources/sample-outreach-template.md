## Usage Notes

- Replace all placeholders before sending.
- Do not include any statement that references a protected characteristic (age, religion, family status, etc.) in any candidate-facing message, including rejections.
- Personalize the bracketed reasoning fields with role-specific, factual detail — avoid generic or templated language that could be perceived as a form rejection without review.
