## Requisition & Sourcing — Standard Positions

### Requisition Approval

**Standard position:** New requisitions require Hiring Manager submission + HR Business Partner approval before sourcing begins. Backfills are pre-approved; net-new headcount requires VP-level sign-off.

**Acceptable variations:** Expedited approval for critical/urgent roles with CHRO authorization.

**Hard limits:** No sourcing or outreach begins without an approved, open requisition on record.

---

### Panel Composition

**Standard position:** Minimum 3 panelists per role: Hiring Manager, one peer/technical panelist, one cross-functional panelist. Senior/Director+ roles add a skip-level panelist.

**Acceptable variations:** 2-panelist minimum for high-volume/entry-level roles.

**Hard limits:** No single-interviewer hiring decisions for roles above entry level. At least one panelist must be independent of the hiring manager's direct reporting chain.

---

### Time-to-Fill Targets

**Standard position:** 30 days requisition-to-offer for standard individual contributor roles. 45 days for senior/specialist roles. 60+ days for executive search.

**Acceptable variations:** Targets adjusted for hard-to-fill or niche-skill roles; document rationale in the requisition.

**Hard limits:** Requisitions open beyond 90 days require pipeline review with HR leadership.

---

## Interviewing & Evaluation — Standard Positions

### Competency Alignment

**Standard position:** All interview questions and scorecards must map to the role's approved competency framework. No ad hoc or off-framework evaluation criteria.

**Acceptable variations:** Role-specific technical competencies may be added with Hiring Manager + HRBP sign-off.

**Hard limits:** No evaluation on protected characteristics (age, race, gender, religion, disability, national origin, etc.) under any circumstance.

---

### Scorecard Submission

**Standard position:** Each panelist submits an independent scorecard within 24 hours of interview completion, before group discussion.

**Acceptable variations:** 48-hour window for panelists across multiple time zones.

**Hard limits:** No consensus discussion begins until all panelists have submitted independent scorecards.

---

### Candidate Communication

**Standard position:** Candidates receive acknowledgment within 2 business days of application and a decision (advance/reject) within 5 business days of final interview.

**Acceptable variations:** Extended timelines communicated proactively to the candidate if panel scheduling requires it.

**Hard limits:** No candidate left without a status update for more than 10 business days.

---

## Offer & Closing — Standard Positions

### Offer Approval Thresholds

**Standard position:** Offers within approved salary band — Hiring Manager + HRBP approval. Offers above band — VP approval required. Exception offers (sign-on bonus above standard, accelerated equity) — CHRO approval required.

**Acceptable variations:** Pre-approved bands for competitive/critical roles on record with Finance.

**Hard limits:** No verbal or written offer extended without HRBP sign-off. No compensation disclosed to a candidate that has not been approved.

---

### Rejection & Candidate Experience

**Standard position:** All rejected candidates receive a professional, role-specific notification. No generic feedback on protected characteristics or unverified claims about the candidate's fit.

**Acceptable variations:** Personalized feedback may be offered for candidates who reached final-round interviews, at the Hiring Manager's discretion.

**Hard limits:** No rejection reason that references a protected characteristic. No sharing of other candidates' information.
