## Level Definitions

| Level | Title Band | Years of Experience (typical) | Scope |
|-------|-----------|-------------------------------|-------|
| L1 | Associate / Entry | 0–2 | Executes defined tasks with guidance |
| L2 | Professional | 2–5 | Owns a workstream independently |
| L3 | Senior | 5–8 | Owns a function or complex project; mentors others |
| L4 | Staff / Lead | 8–12 | Sets direction for a team or domain |
| L5 | Principal / Director | 12+ | Sets strategy across multiple teams or functions |

## Core Competencies (apply to all roles)

### Communication

**L1–L2:** Communicates clearly in routine, structured situations.
**L3–L4:** Adapts communication style to audience; influences without authority.
**L5:** Communicates strategy and vision to executive stakeholders.

### Problem Solving

**L1–L2:** Solves well-defined problems using established methods.
**L3–L4:** Solves ambiguous problems; identifies root causes.
**L5:** Frames problems at the organizational level; anticipates second-order effects.

### Collaboration

**L1–L2:** Works effectively within own team.
**L3–L4:** Partners cross-functionally; resolves competing priorities.
**L5:** Builds alignment across senior stakeholders and functions.

### Ownership

**L1–L2:** Delivers assigned tasks reliably.
**L3–L4:** Drives outcomes proactively without being asked.
**L5:** Owns business-level outcomes and accountability.

## Role-Family Competencies — Example: Software Engineering

### Technical Depth

**L1–L2:** Implements well-specified features with code review support.
**L3–L4:** Designs systems; makes sound architectural trade-offs.
**L5:** Sets technical direction across multiple systems or teams.

### Quality & Reliability

**L1–L2:** Writes tested, maintainable code.
**L3–L4:** Establishes quality practices for a team.
**L5:** Establishes engineering standards across the organization.

---

## Role-Family Competencies — Example: Sales & Marketing

### Market/Customer Knowledge

**L1–L2:** Understands assigned territory or campaign segment.
**L3–L4:** Anticipates market shifts; advises on positioning.
**L5:** Shapes go-to-market strategy.

### Execution & Results

**L1–L2:** Meets individual quota/campaign targets.
**L3–L4:** Consistently exceeds targets; coaches others.
**L5:** Owns revenue or pipeline strategy across a region or product line.

## Notes for Interview Kit & Scorecard Generation

- Map every interview question to one of the competencies above.
- Use a consistent rating scale across panelists (recommended: 1–4, where 1 = Does Not Meet, 2 = Partially Meets, 3 = Meets, 4 = Exceeds).
- Scorecards should reference the specific level the candidate is being evaluated against (e.g., evaluating an L3 candidate against L3 expectations, not L4).
